## Project Overview

This project analyzes HR data to uncover insights into workforce demographics, employee performance, engagement, satisfaction, and training effectiveness. Using Microsoft Excel, I built interactive dashboards with KPI cards, Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers to transform raw employee data into meaningful insights. The final solution supports data-driven HR decision-making through clear and interactive reporting.

## Business Problem
Human Resource departments often manage large volumes of employee data but struggle to extract meaningful insights from it.
Without proper reporting, organizations may find it difficult to:

- Key workforce trends may be difficult to identify from raw HR data.
- Performance, engagement, and training outcomes may not be effectively monitored.
- Departments requiring attention may go unnoticed.
- Training effectiveness may be hard to measure.
- HR decisions may lack data-driven support.

This project addresses these challenges by creating a centralized HR analytics solution.

## Project Objectives
The primary objectives of this project include:
- Analyze workforce demographics and employee distribution.
- Evaluate performance, engagement, satisfaction, and work-life balance metrics.
- Measure training effectiveness, participation, and completion rates.
- Identify key workforce trends and high-performing departments.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making through interactive dashboards.
