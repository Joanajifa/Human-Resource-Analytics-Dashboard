## Project Overview

This project analyzes HR data to uncover insights into workforce demographics, employee performance, engagement, satisfaction, and training effectiveness. Using Microsoft Excel, I built interactive dashboards with KPI cards, Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers to transform raw employee data into meaningful insights. The final solution supports data-driven HR decision-making through clear and interactive reporting.

## Business Problem
Human Resource departments often manage large volumes of employee data but struggle to extract meaningful insights from it.
Without proper reporting, organizations may find it difficult to:

- Key workforce trends may be difficult to identify from raw HR data.
- Performance, engagement, and training outcomes may not be effectively monitored.
- Departments requiring attention may go unnoticed.
- Training effectiveness may be hard to measure.
- HR decisions may lack data-driven support.

This project addresses these challenges by creating a centralized HR analytics solution.

## Project Objectives
The primary objectives of this project include:
- Analyze workforce demographics and employee distribution.
- Evaluate performance, engagement, satisfaction, and work-life balance metrics.
- Measure training effectiveness, participation, and completion rates.
- Identify key workforce trends and high-performing departments.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making through interactive dashboards.

## Calculated Columns
Several calculated columns were created to enhance the analysis and support dashboard reporting
| Tools | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|
| Age Group | 	Categorized employees into Adult, Middle Age , Old and Young Adult for demographic analysis. |
| Tenure  | 	 Measures the number of years employees have spent based on their employment start date. |
| Performance Category | Classified employees as Good, Needs Improvement and Excellent based on performance ratings. |
| Engagement Level | 	Created using high, low and medium to classify employees into different engagement categories. |
| Satisfaction Level | 	Segmented employees based on satisfaction scores to identify sentiment trends. |
| Training Completion Status | 	Created to evaluate the effectiveness of employee training programs by comparing training outcomes and performance indicators. |

### Key Insights
Some of the insights generated from the analysis include:
- Departments with the highest employee concentration.
- Variations in engagement and satisfaction levels across business units.
- Identification of high-performing departments.
- Training programs with the highest participation rates.
- Training outcomes and completion trends.
- Areas where additional employee development initiatives may be required.

## Recommendations
- Increase investment in training programs with high completion and success rates
- Investigate departments with lower engagement or satisfaction scores.
- Develop retention strategies for critical workforce segments.
- Monitor performance metrics regularly to support talent management decisions.
- Use dashboard insights to guide future workforce planning initiatives.
